## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **SILENT BREATH**. This trainer provides optional tools for exploring the forest, managing microphone-based scream risks, controlling hostile AI behavior, and navigating randomized missing-person locations. It is designed for experimentation, accessibility, achievement preparation, and relaxed single-player sessions.

**Target Audience:** Players who want to explore the forest at their own pace, practice against dynamic scares, reduce microphone-related frustration, locate randomized objectives, prepare achievements, or test the game's environments without repeated losses.

## ⚡ Features (v1.0)

### 🎯 Gameplay

- **Scream Protection** — Prevents microphone-triggered scream penalties from ending the current run.
- **Flashlight Control** — Keeps the flashlight active or allows its state to be changed without relying on the normal toggle.
- **Movement Multiplier** — Adjusts walking and sprinting speed for faster navigation across the forest.

### 📌 Survival

- **Threat Immunity** — Protects the player from being caught by hostile entities hunting through the forest.
- **Freeze Hostile AI** — Pauses active threat behavior while leaving normal exploration available.

### 📌 Exploration

- **Missing-Person Locator** — Highlights the current locations of missing people, including locations reshuffled after a failed run.
- **Teleport to Discovered Locations** — Moves the player to supported landmarks and exploration areas without retracing the entire route.

### 📌 Accessibility

- **Jump-Scare Training Mode** — Allows players to practice navigation and threat responses with selected survival penalties disabled.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

SILENT BREATH is played from a first-person perspective and focuses on walking, looking around, sprinting, and careful movement through a large forest. Use the movement multiplier for quicker traversal, and use teleport options only after confirming that the destination is supported by the current game build.

### Tools & Inventory

The game is not centered on a traditional loot or equipment inventory. Progress mainly comes from exploring locations, finding missing people, interacting with important objects, and using the flashlight. Trainer options therefore focus on objective tracking, flashlight behavior, and navigation rather than ammunition or item duplication.

### World & Environment

The forest contains multiple areas and landmarks, while dynamic events and missing-person placements make each run unpredictable. After a loss, the game restarts and previously found missing-person locations can change. The locator and teleport functions are intended to help study these randomized layouts without removing the underlying exploration system.

### Combat & Weapons

SILENT BREATH has no conventional weapon-based combat system. Survival depends on avoiding or outlasting threats, responding to entity behavior, staying aware of the surroundings, and controlling noise picked up by the microphone. Threat Immunity and Freeze Hostile AI replace combat-oriented trainer functions.
